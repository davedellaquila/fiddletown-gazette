How to place an ad

Email the paper with “Gazette classified” in the subject — a headline, two or three lines about the item, the price (or “free”), your town, and a photo if you have one. Ads are free — there’s no charge at all, for the listing or the renewal. An ad runs seven days, and if the item hasn’t moved, the poster can renew it once for seven more. Every ad is read by the desk before it prints, and the desk checks that what’s offered is genuinely local. Your email and phone never appear in the paper: readers reply through the Gazette and the desk passes word along. Categories: For Sale · Free Stuff · Services Offered · Wanted · Lost & Found.
