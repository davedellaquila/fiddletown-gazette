# Publishing — The Fiddletown Gazette

## Rhythm
- **Morning Edition 6:00 AM PT** — reads the Event Buddy intake files on disk at run time. (Time corrected Nov 1, 2026 at Dave's word: the playbook's old ~7:00 AM was stale; the cron runs at 06:00.)
- **Midday Edition ~12:30 PM PT.**
- **Evening Edition ~5:30 PM PT** — evening events + overnight alert picture.
- **Hourly safety poll** between editions (NWS / CHP / CAL FIRE / Cal OES / PG&E). Qualifying events — Red Flag Warning, evacuation order/warning, county fire start, major road closure — trigger an out-of-cycle banner in `#safety-banner` on the front page, stamped with its time.
- Each edition is a full front-page rebuild; each is archived under `editions/YYYY-MM-DD-<morning|midday|evening>.html` and linked from `archive.html`. **Snapshots are frozen as-run** — content is never edited after publication; corrections ride as appended notices. **One recorded exception (Dave's explicit ruling, Nov 6, 2026):** the four draggable-era snapshots (Nov 5 Morning/Midday/Evening + Nov 6 Morning) received a behavior-only code patch — the ticker's `touch-action` changed from `pan-y` to `pan-x pan-y` so their tapes slide on touchscreens, matching the live page's fix the same day. No content, wording, or layout changed; Dave authorized it knowingly as an exception to the freeze, so the archive's history stays honest. Any future patch to a frozen snapshot needs the same kind of explicit ruling, recorded here the same way. **Second recorded repair (Nov 6, 2026, Dave's bug report — "404 when I clicked the Editions Archive link in an archived edition"):** the archive step had copied the front page into `editions/` WITHOUT rebasing its root-relative URLs, so from inside every snapshot, the archive/front-page/recipes/sightings links, ALL photos and videos, the star-chart image, and the icon/manifest links resolved inside `editions/` and 404'd — the snapshots had been silently broken since launch (their text read fine; only clicks and media failed). Repaired in place in all 19 snapshots (attribute values only, content untouched) and prevented going forward: **the archive step now runs `tools/fix_snapshot_links.py <snapshot>` over the snapshot copy before pushing** — it prepends `../` to root-relative `href`/`src`/`poster` values (never `#` anchors, absolute URLs, or already-rebased values; idempotent). An archive push that skips the rebase re-breaks every link in that snapshot. **Edition thumbnails (Dave, Nov 5, 2026):** every `archive.html` entry carries a thumbnail of that edition's front page — a real 480×360 capture of the edition's top (masthead + dateline + weather banner), saved as `editions/thumbs/<same-name>.jpg` and shown with the `.ed-thumb` treatment — **a FLOAT layout (Dave, Nov 6, 2026: "make the thumbnail a little larger and have the text wrap around it")**: the thumbnail floats left at 168×126 desktop / 118×92 phone and each entry's summary text wraps around it, running full width beneath; `.arch-entry` is a plain block with a clearfix (NOT flex — flex is what kept the text in a column beside the thumbnail), the `.ed-thumb-link` floats with a small right/bottom margin, and the image itself links to the edition, alt = "Front page of the <edition name>". Capture it in the same push that archives the edition (render the archived HTML and screenshot its top). **Capture route (settled Nov 6, 2026):** the sandbox's headless Chromium is policy-blocked for direct agent shells (Hatch WebGL domain policy) — that is why the Nov 5 Evening and Nov 6 Morning captures failed in their edition runs and those entries went in text-only. The standing route is `tools/render_thumb.py <snapshot.html> <thumbs/same-name.jpg>` in the paper repo: a deterministic renderer that draws the edition's actual top (masthead + real fiddle watermark + dateline + weather bug + ticker line + lead headline + the snapshot's own five-day outlook values) at the same 960-wide composition. Edition jobs: if a browser capture is unavailable in your environment, run the renderer — never ship a text-only entry for want of a browser, and never fake a capture from another edition's data. An entry whose thumbnail isn't on file stays text-only — never a placeholder or stock image (same rule as the Sightings Archive photos). **Archive summaries are a few sentences at most (Dave, Nov 6, 2026 — "no more than several sentences… about twice as big as it needs to be"):** the archive entry is a browsing aid, not a digest — the edition's own lede plus one or two headline facts a reader needs to decide whether to open it (the day's events in brief, weather/roads gist, any edition-defining note such as a correction or a section change), roughly 200–350 characters all-in. The as-run bookkeeping (sweep-by-sweep detail, per-source reads, carried-forward notes, thumbnail-capture apologies) belongs in the edition report, NOT the archive entry. All 19 launch-week entries were brought to this standard Nov 6, 2026. The masthead always shows when the page was last rebuilt; every item carries its timestamp and source.
- **About Fiddletown (`about-fiddletown.html`, opened Nov 5, 2026 at Dave's direction):** the paper's standing page for the town itself — its landmarks and history, kept current as the record grows; it carries the full-screen photo lightbox like every newspaper page. ## Pre-publish privacy gate (every item, every edition — all three must pass)
1. Public source?
2. Public event? (for events: `isPublic = true`)
3. No private names, addresses, or details? (No residents' private data, no home addresses, nothing from Nextdoor, Dave's mail, or Dave's calendar.)

If any answer is wrong, the item does not print. This line is from Guardian Buddy's Oct 25, 2026 audit and does not bend.

## Standing treatment — Weather Events (added Oct 31, 2026, Dave's word: "obviously the weather events")
- Weather *events* are news, not just a forecast box: storm systems moving in, first rain/snow of the season, heat and freeze events, burn-day status changes, wind events.
- When a weather event is in progress or credibly forecast, the edition carries it as a news item in the main flow (Lead or Around Town, as its importance earns) in addition to the Weather box.
- **Safety-relevant weather events lead the Safety Wire**, as chartered (Red Flag, storm damage risk, chain control, PSPS-relevant wind, flood risk). The hourly safety poll treats a newly-issued severe weather alert as qualifying.
- Weather events *develop* between editions (unlike the history/sky boxes): each edition updates the coverage — rain totals, clearing timing, chain-control changes — with fresh timestamps.
- Worked example, Oct 31, 2026: the season's first real autumn storm system (rain + afternoon thunderstorms, chain control on SR-4) leads the launch editions.
