## National / world page
- **1859 — Darwin publishes *On the Origin of Species* (Nov 24)** — John Murray publishes Charles Darwin's book in London, in an edition of 1,250 copies; it sets out the theory of evolution by natural selection, formed over the two decades since the *Beagle* voyage.
- **1971 — D.B. Cooper hijacks Flight 305 (Nov 24)** — a hijacker calling himself "Dan Cooper" parachutes from a Northwest Orient Airlines 727 over the Pacific Northwest after receiving $200,000 in ransom; his fate remains unknown.
- **1963 — Jack Ruby shoots Lee Harvey Oswald (Nov 24)** — in the basement of Dallas police headquarters, nightclub owner Jack Ruby shoots Oswald, the accused assassin of President Kennedy, in a scene captured on live television.
- **1974 — "Lucy" is found in Ethiopia (Nov 24)** — Donald Johanson and Tom Gray discover a largely complete *Australopithecus afarensis* skeleton in the Afar Depression; named Lucy, she rewrites the human family tree.
