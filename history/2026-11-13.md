## HIGHLIGHT — California page (gold panel)
- The desk dug back to 1848 for a Nov 13 event in Fiddletown or Amador County itself in the Gold Rush years — **none could be verified**. The box says so plainly; no date is stretched.
- California highlight: **1849 — California voters ratify the state's first constitution (Nov 13), 177 years ago today.** The convention at Colton Hall, Monterey, finished its work Oct 13, 1849; on Nov 13 the people adopted the constitution by a vote of 12,872 to 811 (California Legislature, ACR-121). It prohibited slavery, gave married women the right to own property, required laws published in English and Spanish, and named San Jose the first capital — the foundation for admission to the Union on Sep 9, 1850. Contemporary accounts describe a rain-soaked vote in San Francisco, the mud "unfathomable" — a fair echo of this morning's storm in the foothills. Sources: California Legislature ACR-121 text (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov); Colton Hall marker (HMdb); CalEXPLORnia "This Week in California History" (the rain/mud detail); checked Nov 13, 2026.

## California / national page
- **1940 — Disney's *Fantasia* premieres (Nov 13)** as a roadshow at the Broadway Theatre in New York — the film that grew out of *The Sorcerer's Apprentice*; later named to the National Film Registry (1990).
- **1982 — the Vietnam Veterans Memorial is dedicated in Washington, D.C. (Nov 13).** Maya Lin's black-granite Wall, bearing more than 58,000 names, was dedicated during a weeklong national salute to Vietnam veterans.
