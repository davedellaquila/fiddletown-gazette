## HIGHLIGHT — Mother Lode / Gold Country tie (gold panel)
- The desk dug back to 1848 for a Nov 9 event in Fiddletown or Amador County itself in the Gold Rush years — **none could be verified**. The box says so plainly; no date is stretched.
- Gold Country highlight: **Lotta Crabtree** — the Gold Rush's child star, who as a girl sang and danced for miners in the Sierra gold camps (taken under Lola Montez's wing in Grass Valley) and grew up to be one of the most famous American actresses of the 19th century — made her **final public appearance on Nov 9, 1915**, during the Panama-Pacific International Exposition festivities in San Francisco. Source: CalExplornia — California History in November (November 9 entry), checked Nov 9, 2026.

## California page
- **1967 — Rolling Stone publishes its first issue (Nov 9).** Founded in San Francisco by Jann Wenner and Ralph J. Gleason; John Lennon on the cover. Source: CalExplornia, November 9.
- **1969 — Native American activists reach Alcatraz (Nov 9).** An early attempt to claim the abandoned federal island under treaty provisions on unused federal land; brief, but it foreshadowed the full occupation that began Nov 20, 1969. Source: CalExplornia, November 9.

## National / world page — Nov 9
- **1989 — The Berlin Wall falls (Nov 9).** East Germany announces citizens may cross freely; crowds swarm the Wall that night, ending 28 years of division.
- **1938 — Kristallnacht (Nov 9–10).** The Nazi pogrom against Jewish communities across Germany and Austria — synagogues burned, businesses destroyed, thousands arrested. A solemn anniversary.
- **1965 — The Northeast blackout (Nov 9).** A cascading grid failure leaves some 30 million people across the Northeast and Ontario without power.
