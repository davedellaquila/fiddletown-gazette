## HIGHLIGHT — Mother Lode / Gold Country, post–Gold Rush era
- **1883 — Black Bart's last stagecoach robbery (Nov 3).** Charles E. Boles, the masked "poet outlaw" Black Bart, holds up his 28th and last stage — the Sonora-to-Milton coach — on Funk Hill, southeast of Copperopolis in Calaveras County, the southern Mother Lode, at the very spot of his first robbery in 1875. The Wells Fargo strongbox is bolted to the floor; while Bart hacks at it, 19-year-old passenger Jimmy Rolleri, who had stepped off to hunt, fires and wounds Bart in the hand. Bart flees with the gold (about $4,100 in amalgam and $550 in gold coin, per one account) but leaves behind a handkerchief whose laundry mark Wells Fargo detectives trace to him. He confesses, pleads guilty to this one robbery, and goes to San Quentin. Sources: Legends of America — Black Bart · Historical Crime Detective (Thomas Duke, 1910) · Wikipedia, Black Bart (outlaw) · Calaveras County Historical Society via blackbart.com.

## California page
- **1997 — Proposition 209 stands (Nov 3).** The U.S. Supreme Court lets stand California's groundbreaking Proposition 209, banning race and gender preferences in public hiring and school admissions. Source: AP Today in History, Nov 3.

## Wider page — a ballot-box date
Nov 3 is repeatedly Election Day in American history:
- **1868** — Ulysses S. Grant elected president.
- **1896** — William McKinley elected president over William Jennings Bryan.
- **1908** — William Howard Taft elected president.
- **1936** — Franklin D. Roosevelt re-elected in a landslide.
- **1964** — Lyndon B. Johnson elected in his own right.
- **1992** — Bill Clinton elected the 42nd president; Carol Moseley Braun becomes the first Black woman elected to the U.S. Senate.
- **2020** — The Biden–Trump election is held (result not known for three days).
- **1911** — Chevrolet Motor Car Co. founded in Detroit by Louis Chevrolet and William C. Durant.
- **1957** — Sputnik 2 carries Laika, the first animal to orbit Earth.
- **2014** — One World Trade Center opens in New York.
