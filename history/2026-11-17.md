## California page
- **1855 — Charles Cora shoots U.S. Marshal William H. Richardson (Nov 17, San Francisco)** — a quarrel in Gold Rush San Francisco ends with the federal marshal shot dead; Cora's case becomes a flashpoint that helps bring on the Vigilance Committee of 1856.
- **1882 — The great aurora (Nov 17)** — one of the strongest geomagnetic storms on record pushes brilliant auroras as far south as San Diego; telegraph lines spark and fail across the country.
- **2003 — Arnold Schwarzenegger is sworn in as California's 38th governor (Nov 17)** — after winning the recall election, he takes the oath in Sacramento.
