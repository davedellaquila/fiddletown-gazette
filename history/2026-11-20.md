## California page
- **1818 — Bouchard enters Monterey Bay (Nov 20)** — Captain Hippolyte Bouchard, sailing for the United Provinces of the Rio de la Plata (Argentina), enters Monterey Bay with two ships; the attack on Spanish California's capital follows, and Monterey is held for days — the only time California was invaded and its capital sacked by a foreign force.
- **1969 — The Occupation of Alcatraz begins (Nov 20)** — Native American activists land on Alcatraz Island in San Francisco Bay and seize the former federal prison, holding it until June 11, 1971 — a landmark of the Native American civil-rights movement.
- **1982 — "The Play" at Berkeley (Nov 20)** — Cal beats Stanford on a five-lateral kickoff return through the Stanford band, which had wandered onto the field early — the most famous finish in college football.

## National / world page
- **1945 — The Nuremberg trials begin (Nov 20)** — trials of 24 Nazi war criminals open at the Palace of Justice in Nuremberg, Germany.
- **1820 — A sperm whale sinks the Essex (Nov 20)** — an 80-ton sperm whale attacks and sinks the Nantucket whaler Essex 2,000 miles off South America — an incident that partly inspired Melville's Moby-Dick.
- **1789 — New Jersey first to ratify the Bill of Rights (Nov 20)** — New Jersey becomes the first U.S. state to ratify the Bill of Rights.
