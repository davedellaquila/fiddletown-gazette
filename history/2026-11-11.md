## California / national page
- **1919 — the first Armistice Day (Nov 11).** President Woodrow Wilson proclaims the first commemoration of Armistice Day, a year after the guns fell silent.
- **1954 — Armistice Day becomes Veterans Day (law approved June 1, 1954).** After World War II and Korea, Congress amends the 1938 act — striking "Armistice," inserting "Veterans" (Public Law 380) — so Nov 11 honors American veterans of all wars. President Eisenhower issues the first Veterans Day Proclamation that October. (A 1968 law briefly moved the observance to October; a 1975 law returned it to Nov 11 from 1978.)
- **1889 — Washington becomes the 42nd state (Nov 11).**
- **1620 — the Mayflower Compact is signed (Nov 11, Old Style)** aboard the Mayflower off Cape Cod.
