- California highlight: **1936 — the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge opens to traffic (Nov 12), ninety years ago today.** Conceived as early as the Gold Rush days; "Emperor" Joshua Norton decreed a bay bridge in 1872. Opened at 12:30 PM — Governor Frank Merriam cut gold chains across the lanes with an acetylene torch; former President Herbert Hoover attended. It opened six months before the Golden Gate Bridge, ahead of schedule and under budget at about $77 million. Sources: California State Archives exhibit (Google Arts & Culture, "Spanning the Bay"); Britannica, Bay Bridge; checked Nov 12, 2026.

## California / national page
- **1958 — first ascent of The Nose on El Capitan completed (Nov 12).** Warren Harding, Wayne Merry and George Whitmore top out in Yosemite after 47 days of climbing spread over some 18 months — the first ascent of El Capitan's most famous route.
- **1980 — Voyager 1 makes its closest approach to Saturn (Nov 12)** and takes the first detailed images of its rings. (A sky tie: Saturn is tonight's evening planet — see the Star Chart.)
