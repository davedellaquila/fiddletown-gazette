- **1907 — Oklahoma becomes the 46th state (Nov 16)** — Oklahoma Territory and Indian Territory join as one state; President Theodore Roosevelt signs the proclamation.
- **1914 — The Federal Reserve Banks open (Nov 16)** — the twelve regional Federal Reserve Banks open for business.
- **1945 — UNESCO is founded (Nov 16)** — representatives sign the constitution of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization in London.
- **1973 — Skylab 4 launches, and the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Act is signed (Nov 16)** — NASA's third and final Skylab crew lifts off for an 84-day mission, then the longest American spaceflight; the same day, President Nixon signs the act authorizing the Alaska Pipeline.
