## California page
- **1883 — Standard time reaches California (Nov 18)** — North America's railroads adopt four standard time zones at noon, the "day of two noons," ending thousands of local times; California towns and railroads reset their clocks to Pacific standard time.
- **1978 — Jonestown (Nov 18)** — more than 900 members of the Peoples Temple — a movement founded in Indiana but built in California, headquartered in San Francisco with a commune at Redwood Valley — die in Guyana in a mass murder-suicide directed by Jim Jones; U.S. Rep. Leo Ryan of California is killed at the Port Kaituma airstrip.

## National / world page
- **1928 — Steamboat Willie is released (Nov 18)** — Walt Disney's Mickey Mouse makes his sound-era debut in the first fully synchronized sound cartoon.
- **1963 — The first push-button telephone (Nov 18)** — Bell Telephone introduces Touch-Tone push-button dialing, beginning the end of the rotary dial.
- **1985 — Calvin and Hobbes debuts (Nov 18)** — Bill Watterson's comic strip about a boy and his tiger appears in newspapers for the first time.
- **2026 — Voyager 1 reaches a full light-day (about Nov 18)** — NASA's Voyager 1, launched in 1977, becomes the first human-made object whose radio signals take a full 24 hours to reach Earth.
