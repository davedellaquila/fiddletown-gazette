## HIGHLIGHT — living-history tie (gold panel)

- Living-history highlight: **the United States Marine Corps was founded on Nov 10, 1775** — the Second Continental Congress resolved to raise two battalions of Continental Marines; tradition places the first recruiting at Tun Tavern, Philadelphia, under Capt. Samuel Nicholas. Today is the Corps' **251st birthday**, and it is being marked **in the beat tonight**: the Marine Corps Birthday Ball at Castle Oaks Golf Course, Ione, 5:00–10:00 PM (see Today in the Foothills). ## California / national page
- **1969 — Sesame Street premieres (Nov 10).** The Children's Television Workshop's show, created by Joan Ganz Cooney and Lloyd Morrisett with Jim Henson's Muppets, debuts on National Educational Television and remakes children's television.
- **1958 — The Hope Diamond goes to the Smithsonian (Nov 10).** New York jeweler Harry Winston donates the 45.52-carat blue diamond, mailing it to the museum in a plain package.
- **1871 — Stanley meets Livingstone (Nov 10).** Explorer Henry Morton Stanley reaches missionary David Livingstone at Ujiji, on Lake Tanganyika — "Dr. Livingstone, I presume?"
- **1975 — The SS Edmund Fitzgerald sinks (Nov 10).** The ore carrier goes down in a Lake Superior storm with all 29 hands — later sung by Gordon Lightfoot.
