# This Day in History — Thursday, November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving Day)

Placement: MAIN column, after The Week Ahead (`#history`). Set by the Morning Edition; Midday/Evening carry forward unchanged.

**Local check (honest):** the desk dug back to 1848 through Gold Country timelines, Amador County histories, and Mother Lode records and found no verified Fiddletown, Amador County, or Mother Lode event dated November 26. The gold panel says so plainly — and notes the date itself is the local story: Thanksgiving lands on Nov 26 this year, the very date Lincoln fixed for it in 1863.

**Items:**
- **1863 — Lincoln proclaims November 26 a national Thanksgiving Day** — President Abraham Lincoln proclaims Nov 26, 1863 as a national day of Thanksgiving, to be celebrated annually on the final Thursday of November. Thanksgiving Day 2026 falls on Nov 26 — the same date, 163 years on.
- **1789 — Washington's first Thanksgiving is observed (Nov 26)** — the first national Thanksgiving under the new Constitution is observed, as proclaimed by President George Washington at Congress's request.
- **1842 — The University of Notre Dame is founded (Nov 26)** — Father Edward Sorin and the Congregation of Holy Cross found the university at South Bend, Indiana.
- **1922 — Carter enters Tutankhamun's tomb (Nov 26)** — Howard Carter and Lord Carnarvon become the first people in more than 3,000 years to enter the burial chamber of Pharaoh Tutankhamun in Egypt's Valley of the Kings.
- **1942 — *Casablanca* premieres in New York (Nov 26)** — the Humphrey Bogart–Ingrid Bergman film premieres at the Hollywood Theatre in New York City.
- **1865 — *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* published in America (Nov 26)** — Lewis Carroll's book, published in Britain earlier that year, reaches American readers; it has never been out of print since.
- **2011 — NASA launches the Curiosity rover (Nov 26)** — the Mars Science Laboratory lifts off from Cape Canaveral carrying Curiosity, which lands in Gale Crater the following August and works on Mars still.
