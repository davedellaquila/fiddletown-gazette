## California page
- **1970 — Conservatory of Flowers becomes a state landmark (Nov 19)** — San Francisco's Golden Gate Park greenhouse, built in 1879 and the state's first municipal greenhouse, is designated California Historical Landmark No. 841.
- **1939 — Joe DiMaggio marries in San Francisco (Nov 19)** — the Yankee Clipper, raised in San Francisco, weds Dorothy Arnold at Sts. Peter and Paul Church as thousands crowd the streets outside.
- **1954 — Sammy Davis Jr. is hurt near San Bernardino (Nov 19)** — the entertainer is badly injured in a car crash on Route 66; he loses the sight in his left eye — the mishap he later called the turning point of his life.
- **1990 — Milli Vanilli's Grammy is revoked (Nov 19)** — the Recording Academy takes back the duo's Best New Artist award — the first Grammy ever rescinded — after producer Frank Farian reveals the pair did not sing on their records; consumer suits follow, including one filed in Alameda County.

## National / world page
- **1863 — The Gettysburg Address (Nov 19)** — President Abraham Lincoln dedicates the Soldiers' National Cemetery at Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, in about two minutes of remarks that reframe the Civil War.
- **1969 — Apollo 12 lands on the Moon (Nov 19)** — Pete Conrad and Alan Bean touch down in the Ocean of Storms, the second crewed Moon landing, walking distance from the Surveyor 3 probe.
- **1985 — Reagan meets Gorbachev (Nov 19)** — the U.S. and Soviet leaders meet face to face for the first time, in Geneva — the start of the summitry that ends the Cold War.
