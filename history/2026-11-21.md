## California page
- **1925 — Lava Beds National Monument is established (Nov 21)** — President Calvin Coolidge establishes Lava Beds National Monument in northeastern California — lava-tube caves and Captain Jack's Stronghold (Modoc War).
- **1969 — The first permanent ARPANET link (Nov 21)** — UCLA and SRI establish a permanent network connection, an early stitch in the internet.
- **1910 — Attempted piracy off San Francisco (Nov 21)** — attempted seizure aboard the steamship Buckman near San Francisco by men who reportedly believed she carried gold; the captain was killed. (CalExplornia.)
