## HIGHLIGHT — California / Gold Rush era (honestly labeled; no verified in-beat Nov 6 item)
- **1860 — Lincoln elected, and California votes with him (Nov 6).** On Tuesday, Nov 6, 1860, Abraham Lincoln is elected the 16th President — the first Republican president — with about 40% of the popular vote and 180 electoral votes against Douglas, Breckinridge and Bell. Crucially for this paper's beat: Lincoln **carried California** — a state admitted only ten years earlier, in 1850, on the strength of the Gold Rush — along with Oregon and all the free states. Fiddletown was then an 11-year-old mining town; its voters cast ballots in a state the Gold Rush had made, in an election that pushed the country toward Civil War. Sources: HISTORY, This Day in History — Nov 6 (Lincoln elected president) · Ohio State eHistory, Election of Lincoln (Nov 6, 1860; 180 electoral votes; carried all free states) · Wikipedia, 1860 United States presidential election (date Nov 6, 1860; Lincoln prevailed in 18 states).

## National page — Nov 6 is an Election Day date, again
- **1861 — Jefferson Davis elected Confederate president (Nov 6).** A year to the day after Lincoln's election, voters in the seceded states elect Jefferson Davis president of the Confederate States of America, unopposed, to a six-year term. Source: HISTORY, Nov 6 (noted in the Lincoln item) · Mystic Stamp, This Day in History — Nov 6, 1861.
- **2012 — Barack Obama re-elected (Nov 6).** President Obama wins a second term, defeating Mitt Romney. Source: Nov 6 timelines, checked Nov 6, 2026.
- **1865 — The last Confederate surrender (Nov 6).** The Confederate raider CSS Shenandoah hauls down its flag and surrenders at Liverpool, England — the final surrender of the Civil War, months after Appomattox, by a crew that had only lately learned the war was over.
