## National / world page
- **1867 — Alfred Nobel patents dynamite (Nov 25)** — in Sweden, Alfred Nobel patents his new blasting explosive, dynamite. Safer to handle than straight nitroglycerin, it goes on to reshape mining worldwide — including the hard-rock gold mines of the Mother Lode.
- **1915 — Einstein presents general relativity (Nov 25)** — Albert Einstein presents the final field equations of his general theory of relativity to the Prussian Academy of Sciences in Berlin, completing a decade's work and remaking physics' picture of gravity, space, and time.
- **1783 — Evacuation Day: the last British troops leave New York (Nov 25)** — the final British forces evacuate New York City, ending the military occupation of the new United States; George Washington rides in the same day.
- **1952 — *The Mousetrap* opens in London (Nov 25)** — Agatha Christie's murder mystery opens at the Ambassadors Theatre; it becomes the longest-running play in history.
- **1963 — President Kennedy is laid to rest (Nov 25)** — after the state funeral in Washington, John F. Kennedy is buried at Arlington National Cemetery, and Jacqueline Kennedy lights the eternal flame.
