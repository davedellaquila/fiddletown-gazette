## HIGHLIGHT — California (honestly labeled; no verified in-beat Nov 5 item)
- **1968 — A Californian elected President (Nov 5).** Republican Richard M. Nixon — born in Yorba Linda, raised in California, the state's former U.S. Senator — defeats Vice President Hubert Humphrey in one of the closest elections in U.S. history. The same day, Shirley Chisholm of New York becomes the first Black woman elected to Congress. Sources: HISTORY, This Day in History — Nov 5 · USA Today, Today in History — Nov 5 · checked Nov 5, 2026.

## National page — Nov 5 is an Election Day date
- **1872 — Susan B. Anthony votes (Nov 5).** In Rochester, New York, Anthony and 14 other women cast ballots in the presidential election, 48 years before the 19th Amendment. She was arrested two weeks later, convicted, fined $100 — and famously never paid it. Source: HISTORY, Nov 5; Smithsonian Magazine.
- **1912 — Woodrow Wilson elected (Nov 5).** Wilson wins a four-way race in a landslide — 435 electoral votes to Theodore Roosevelt's 88 and incumbent William Howard Taft's 8. Source: HISTORY, Nov 5.
- **1940 — FDR elected to a third term (Nov 5).** Franklin D. Roosevelt becomes the first (and still only) president elected to a third term. Source: HISTORY, Nov 5.

## Wider page
- **1605 — The Gunpowder Plot is foiled (Nov 5).** Guy Fawkes is found guarding the gunpowder under Parliament in the early hours of Nov 5 — "Remember, remember, the fifth of November." Source: HISTORY, Nov 5.
- **1994 — A former California governor's letter (Nov 5).** Ronald Reagan, former governor of California, releases a handwritten letter announcing his Alzheimer's diagnosis. The same day in Las Vegas, 45-year-old George Foreman becomes the oldest heavyweight champion in boxing history. Source: HISTORY, Nov 5.
