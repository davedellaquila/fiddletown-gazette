🧺 Talk of the Table

A basket of good talk for the holiday table. The holidays gather everyone into one room — and some years the conversation needs a better starting place than the argument nobody wins. So the Gazette has filled a basket for the season: twenty-five topics worth talking about, in five small bundles. It opens for Thanksgiving, stays open to your additions all December, and comes back refreshed for Christmas. Browse them now, draw one at the table, and argue about the duck-sized horses instead. The idea came from Dave and Nan at Cooper Vineyards, the Sunday before Thanksgiving — the desk just filled the basket.

Add yours: email the desk with the subject “Gazette table talk” — your name is optional, and the best submissions join the basket in the next edition. Nothing is stored on this page, and no contact details ever print. And if one of these lands beautifully at your table, email us its number — Thanksgiving Day's edition prints the Most Loved at the Table top five, and Christmas Day does it again with a fresh round: Most Loved at the Christmas Table.

The basket is full
Twenty-five topics, waiting. Draw one when the table goes quiet — or before it does.

Stories & Memories

1. What meal from childhood would you bring back for one night — and who cooked it?

2. Tell about a holiday meal that went sideways — and why you still laugh about it.

3. What's the first house you remember living in? Walk us in through the front door.

4. Who taught you to cook one thing you still make — and can you still hear their instructions?

5. What did your family always argue about at the table when you were a kid — the harmless kind?

Gratitude

6. Name one small thing from this year — not the big stuff — that you're quietly thankful for.

7. Who showed up for you this year in a way you didn't expect?

8. What's something you own that you'd never sell, because of where it came from?

9. Thank the cook properly: what's the best bite you've had this year, anywhere?

10. What's a hard thing from this year that you're — surprisingly — a little grateful for?

Light & Silly

11. Would you rather face one horse-sized duck or twenty duck-sized horses? Defend your answer.

12. If this family had a mascot, what would it be — and who at the table is most like it?

13. Would you rather give up pie forever, or leftovers forever?

14. If Thanksgiving dinner had walk-up songs, what would yours be?

15. One fictional character gets the extra chair at this table. Who?

Generations

16. For the oldest at the table: what did a holiday meal cost, start to finish, when you were ten?

17. For the youngest at the table: what are the grown-ups getting wrong about phones?

18. Oldest to youngest: what's one thing you know how to do that you want to pass on before next year?

19. Youngest to oldest: what's one thing you wish the grown-ups understood about being your age right now?

20. Everyone: what did your grandparents do for fun that sounds impossible now?

Next Year

21. Where's one place within a day's drive this table should go together next year?

22. What's one thing you want to actually learn before next Thanksgiving?

23. If next year's table could gain one more chair, whose would it be?

24. Name one small plan for next year out loud, so this table can hold you to it.

25. Predict one thing about next Thanksgiving — the sillier the better. The record will be checked.
